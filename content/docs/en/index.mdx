Jikū replaces the WhatsApp group, the Excel file and the paper list: your invitations, QR tickets and the door of your events on one side; your bookings and the day line of your services on the other. Your guests and clients install nothing: everything happens on WhatsApp and in the browser.

## Create your account

On **Create your account**, enter your name, your **organization name**, your email and a password: your space opens right away. A confirmation link goes to your address; tap it whenever you like, before sending your first invitations. **Resend the link** sends it again from your space.

## What Jikū costs

Events are free for up to 100 guests a year, the Solo plan is free forever for one person who receives clients, and ticket sales cost 3% per ticket sold. The [pricing page](/en/pricing) gives the exact amount in Guinean francs, CFA francs or dollars, all taxes included.
