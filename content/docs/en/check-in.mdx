---
title: Check guests in
description: One link per door staff, QR tickets that pass only once, even offline.
---

On the day, your door staff scan QR tickets from their phone. They need no account: each one gets a personal link.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Add your operators

In **Operators**, add each person who checks guests in, with a name that tells them apart ("Mamadou, front door"). Each one gets a personal link that only opens what you entrust to them.

![The Operators page, with one door person and their Copy link button](/guides/en/operators.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Send the link

Tap **Copy link** on their row and send it to the door staff, on WhatsApp for example. They open it in their phone's browser: no app to install.

</Step>
<Step>

### Scan the tickets

At the door, they scan each guest's QR ticket. A ticket passes only once: a copy is refused. After **Checked in**, the scanner comes back by itself for the next guest; a refused ticket stays on screen until the validator taps it.

![Door check on a door person's phone, with Scan the QR and Search](/guides/en/checkin.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>

## Offline

The guest list is already on the door staff's phone: checking continues when the network drops, and entries sync when the connection returns. You follow arrivals live from the event.

<Callout type="warn" title="A door staff loses their phone?">
  In **Operators**, open the **…** menu on their row and tap **Revoke**: their link stops working, without touching the others.
</Callout>
