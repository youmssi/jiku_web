---
title: Connect your WhatsApp number
description: Send your invitations and reminders from your own number, for free, with the steps on Meta's side.
---

By default, your WhatsApp messages go out from Jikū's number. You can connect your own WhatsApp Business number instead: your guests and clients see your name, reply to you directly, and your contacts stay yours.

It is **free in every plan**, the free plan included. Jikū charges nothing to connect it. Meta bills you directly for the messages sent from your number, at its own rates.

## Before you start

Have ready:

- **a phone number** that can receive a text or a call, for Meta's confirmation code;
- **a Facebook account** to sign in to Meta;
- **a bank card** you can save with Meta (Visa or Mastercard, a prepaid card accepted for online payments included);
- **your organization's name** as your contacts should see it;
- for the business verification: your **company registration (RCCM)**, a **tax document** and a **proof of address** in the organization's name.

<Callout type="warn" title="Is the number already on WhatsApp?">
  A number already used in the WhatsApp app cannot always be connected as it is. In some countries, Meta offers to keep it in the WhatsApp Business app as well; otherwise, the number's WhatsApp account has to be deleted in the app first. The simplest is a number dedicated to your organization.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the Meta window from Jikū

In **Settings**, under **Messages**, open **Messaging providers**. In **Your WhatsApp number**, tap **Connect my WhatsApp number**. A Meta window opens.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create or pick your Meta accounts

In the Meta window, sign in with Facebook, then pick or create:

- your Meta **business portfolio** (formerly Business Manager);
- your **WhatsApp Business account**;
- your number's **display name**: the one your contacts will see. Meta reviews it, usually within a day or two; it must match your organization's name.

</Step>
<Step>

### Confirm your number

Enter your number, then the code received by text or call. Finish the window: back in Jikū, your number shows as **Connected**.

Jikū then sets up the rest for you: it receives your guests' replies and creates in your account the message templates it uses (invitation, ticket, reminder). Meta reviews each template before accepting it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add a payment method at Meta

In [Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com), open your WhatsApp Business account's settings, **Billing & payments** section, and add your card. Without a valid payment method, Meta refuses your messages: Jikū says so in the message status, and sending resumes as soon as the card is added.

</Step>
<Step>

### Have Meta verify your business

In Meta Business Suite, open the **Security Center** and tap **Start verification**. Meta asks for the legal name, address, a phone number and a website or email address in your name, then your documents (company registration, tax document, proof of address). The answer takes from a few days to two weeks.

This is **Meta's** verification. It is separate from your organization's **Verification** in Jikū (**Settings**, under **Payments**), which is still required before you take payments.

</Step>
<Step>

### Send a test message

Back in **Your WhatsApp number**, use **Send a test message** with your own phone number in international format, for example +224620000000. The message should arrive from your number, with your name.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What changes once connected

- **Your messages go out from your number**, with your name and your WhatsApp Business profile picture.
- **Meta bills you for your messages**: invitations, tickets and reminders. Appointment reminders sent from your number no longer count against your plan's reminder limit.
- **The Jikū number's limit no longer applies to you.** Your number has its own limits at Meta: until the business is verified, Meta caps the messages you start at about 250 different people per 24 hours; after verification, the limit rises step by step as long as your messages keep a good quality rating.
- **Keep a good quality rating**: send informational messages, to people who know you. The guide [Writing a WhatsApp message that goes through](/en/docs/whatsapp-messages) explains how.

## If something gets stuck

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The Meta window does not open | Allow pop-ups for Jikū, check your connection and try again. |
| "This number is already connected to another organization." | A number serves one organization in Jikū. Disconnect it from the other organization, or use another number. |
| "Meta refused the connection." | Start again from **Connect my WhatsApp number** and finish the Meta window. |
| A message mentions a payment method problem | Add or replace the card in Meta Business Suite; sending resumes. |
| The display name is rejected | Change it in Meta Business Suite so it matches your organization's name. |

## Go back to Jikū's number

In **Your WhatsApp number**, tap **Disconnect**. Your messages go out from Jikū's number again right away. Your WhatsApp Business account stays yours at Meta.

<Callout title="Rather not use the Meta window?">
  **Enter my credentials manually** lets you enter the access token and the number ID of a Meta app you already run. For teams that have already set up the WhatsApp Business API.
</Callout>
