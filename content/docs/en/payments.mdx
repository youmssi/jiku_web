---
title: Payments
description: Your clients pay you directly; Jikū never touches their money.
---

Jikū does not collect your clients' money. They pay you on your Mobile Money numbers, your own payment link or in cash, and you mark "paid" in one tap.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Get your organization verified

In **Settings**, under **Payments**, open **Verification** and choose **I organize in my own name** or **I organize for a company or an association**. Only the form for your case shows. Verification is required before you can take payments.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add your payment methods

Still in **Settings**, open **Payment methods** and add your numbers (Orange Money, MTN MoMo…) or your payment link. They show on your clients' tickets and pages.

</Step>
<Step>

### Record payments

When a guest or a client has paid you, mark their ticket as **paid**. You see at a glance who has settled.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Sell tickets

For a concert, a party or a training, open sales: people buy their seat and pay you directly. Jikū takes 3% per ticket sold, nothing if nothing sells, and the first 50 tickets are free.

## Pay Jikū

What you owe Jikū (an event beyond 100 guests a year, a team subscription) is paid from **Billing**. The exact amount shows before you pay.

A week before a team subscription ends, a reminder shows at the top of your space with **Renew**. Without a renewal, a short grace period starts, then your services pause: clients can no longer book or take a line ticket, but appointments already booked stand, your events carry on and you keep access to your space. Everything resumes once the payment is confirmed.
