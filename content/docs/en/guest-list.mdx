---
title: Invite a guest list
description: Import your guests, choose email or WhatsApp, and publish the event.
---

When you already know who to invite (a wedding, a seminar, a general assembly), each guest gets a personal link and answers in one tap.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Add your guests

In the event's **Guests** tab, **import a guest list** from your Excel or CSV file, or add guests one by one. Duplicates are flagged.

</Step>
<Step>

### Set up categories and prices

If your event has several categories (VIP, press, standard), create them with their capacity and, if needed, their price.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose the invitation channel

On the event's **Overview**, tap **Email**, **WhatsApp** or both: the choice is saved at once.

</Step>
<Step>

### Publish

The **Publish the event** checklist checks the name and date, the channel, the categories and the guest list. Once everything is ready, tap **Publish and send invitations**: the event goes live and the invitations go out. **Publish without sending** is there if you would rather invite later from the **Guests** tab. Invitations go out once your email address is confirmed: until then, the event publishes and you send from the **Guests** tab afterwards.

</Step>
</Steps>

## After sending

You follow who answered and who is coming, live: the event's **Overview** shows how many confirmed, points out the guests who haven't received an invitation yet, and **No answer** opens the list of guests to remind. On the event's day, the door figures lead the Overview. The guest opens their short link (`/v/…`) and taps **I'm coming**: their QR ticket shows right away, to present at the door. The same link then opens the ticket directly.

<Callout>
  You can also combine both: a list of named guests and a [card to share](./invitation-card) for everyone else.
</Callout>
