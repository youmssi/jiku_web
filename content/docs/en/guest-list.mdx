---
title: Invite a guest list
description: Import your guests, choose email or WhatsApp to invite them, and publish the event.
---

When you already know who to invite (a wedding, a seminar, a general assembly), each guest gets a personal link and answers in one tap.

<Steps>
<Step>

## Add your guests

In the event's **Guests** tab, **import a guest list** from your Excel or CSV file, or add guests one by one. Duplicates are flagged. An optional **channel** column (`email` or `whatsapp`) sets a guest's channel; without it, the event's rule applies.

![An event's Guests tab, with the imported list and the Add, Import and Export buttons](/guides/en/guests.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

## Set up categories and prices

If your event has several categories (VIP, press, standard), create them with their capacity and, if needed, their price.

</Step>
<Step>

## Choose the invitation channel

On the event's **Overview**, tap **Email**, **WhatsApp** or both: the choice is saved at once.

Even with both channels, each guest gets **one invitation only**. In **Settings**, **Invitations** section, pick the **Primary channel** and keep **Use the other channel if the first one can't be used** on: a guest with no number then gets the email, and a delivery that fails for good is tried once on the other channel.

<Callout>
  For a wedding, a family party or an association, put WhatsApp first: guests read it right away. For a seminar, a school or a company, email first often works better, as guests find the ticket and the calendar invite there.
</Callout>

![An event's Overview: the Get your event ready checklist, with Email and WhatsApp as invitation channels](/guides/en/event-overview.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

## Publish

The **Publish the event** checklist checks the name and date, the channel, the categories and the guest list. Once everything is ready, tap **Publish and send invitations**: the event goes live and the invitations go out. **Publish without sending** is there if you would rather invite later from the **Guests** tab. Invitations go out once your email address is confirmed: until then, the event publishes and you send from the **Guests** tab afterwards.

</Step>
</Steps>

## After sending

You follow who answered and who is coming, live: the event's **Overview** shows how many confirmed, points out the guests who haven't received an invitation yet, and **No answer** opens the list of guests to remind. To remind them, open **Send invitations** in the **Guests** tab: **Remind guests who haven't answered** lets you pick the reminder's channel, and only the guests who haven't answered yet get a new message. On the event's day, the door figures lead the Overview. The guest opens their short link (`/v/…`) and taps **I'm coming**: their QR ticket shows right away, to present at the door, and is also sent on the channel of their invitation. The same link then opens the ticket directly.

## Send from your own WhatsApp

For fifty to a hundred and fifty guests, a message from your number is opened far more than one from an unknown number. In the **Guests** tab of a published event, tap **From my WhatsApp**: Jikū prepares the personal link of every guest who has a number. Tap **Send** next to a guest: WhatsApp opens their chat with the message already written; send it, come back, and move to the next one. The guest is marked **Sent** and the counter moves on.

![The From my WhatsApp window: the Invite and Remind tabs, the progress and a Send button for each guest](/guides/en/own-whatsapp.webp)

These guests count against the event's allowance as a send by Jikū does. Jikū does not write to them itself: when they answer **I'm coming**, their ticket shows on their link and goes by email when they have one. A guest Jikū already invites is not in the list to send.

The **Remind** tab of the same window lists the guests who haven't answered yet: **Remind** opens their chat with a short reminder and the same personal link. A reminder doesn't count against the allowance, and each guest shows when you reminded them. The window opens by itself on the tab with something left to do.

<Callout>
  You can also combine both: a list of named guests and a [card to share](./invitation-card) for everyone else.
</Callout>
