---
title: Bookings and the day line
description: A booking link, WhatsApp or SMS reminders, and a line that calls everyone on their phone.
---

For a clinic, a salon, an agency or a public office: your clients book a slot, get a reminder, and wait for their turn without sitting in the waiting room.

## Set up bookings

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create a service

In **Services**, create a **new service**: a consultation, a haircut, a counter appointment.

</Step>
<Step>

### Share the booking link

The service's **public booking link** can be shared on your networks or shown as a QR code. Your clients pick a free slot, with no account and no call.

</Step>
<Step>

### Turn on reminders

In **Appointment reminders**, a reminder goes out before each appointment on WhatsApp; SMS takes over if WhatsApp fails.

</Step>
</Steps>

## The day line

Appointments and walk-ins wait in one line. Show the **day line entrance QR** at reception: clients scan it, take their own ticket and follow their place live on their phone.

At the counter, your staff open the **day line** from their link, call the next client ("It's your turn, counter 4") and add walk-ins.

<Callout title="How much does it cost?">
  The Solo plan is free forever for one person who receives clients. For a team, the [simulator](/en/simulator) gives the exact price.
</Callout>
