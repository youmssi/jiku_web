For a clinic, a salon, an agency or a public office: your clients book a slot, get a reminder, and wait for their turn without sitting in the waiting room.

## Set up bookings

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create a service

In **Services**, create a **new service**: its name and the length of one appointment are enough. It can be booked at once, with you receiving clients Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00.

</Step>
<Step>

### Share the booking link

On the **Link and hours** page, tap **Copy link** or **Share**. Your clients pick a free slot, with no account and no call. Just below, **Change hours** adjusts them day by day, breaks included, then **Save hours**. Length and price are set in **Settings**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Turn on reminders

In **Appointment reminders**, a reminder goes out before each appointment on WhatsApp. When SMS is offered, tick **If WhatsApp can't deliver, send an SMS** for clients without WhatsApp or a data connection.

</Step>
</Steps>

## The service card

In the service's **Share** tab, Jikū draws a card to post in your WhatsApp statuses and groups: the service's name, price, length, place and a QR code that leads to booking.

- Fill in **Place shown on the card** then **Save**: the card updates.
- **Share on WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the link ready to send, **Download the card** saves the image, **Copy the link** copies it.
- **Where the card leads**: **Presentation** shows the service, then the times; **Time** opens straight on the first free day, and the card lists the next 3 free times, worked out again on every download. With no free time this week, the page falls back to the presentation with "No times this week".
- **Results over 30 days** split the opens and bookings that came from the card from those that came from the link. A person counts once a day, and WhatsApp's link preview does not count.

<Callout title="Verified organization">
  Sharing opens once your organization is verified. Until then, the card shows as a preview, with the **Verify my organization** button.
</Callout>

## Questions before the appointment

In the service's **Settings**, the **Questions before the appointment** card holds up to 3 choice questions, each with 2 to 10 answers of at most 20 characters (WhatsApp's limit). The **Preview in WhatsApp** shows the conversation: buttons up to 3 answers, a list beyond.

- An answer can **Lead to** another of your services: a client who picks it is offered that service instead.
- The client answers before seeing the times, on the booking page.
- The answers show on the request and on the appointment in the **day line**, and in **Export appointments** (a CSV file per period, at most 92 days).
- They are erased with the appointment and with the client's details.

## The day line

Appointments and walk-ins wait in one line. Show the **day line entrance QR** (in the service's **Advanced options**) at reception: clients scan it, take their own ticket and follow their place live on their phone.

At the counter, your staff open the **day line** from their link, call the next client ("It's your turn, counter 4") and add walk-ins.

</Callout>
