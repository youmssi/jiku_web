title: Bookings and the day line
description: A booking link, WhatsApp reminders, and a line that calls everyone on their phone.
---

For a clinic, a salon, an agency or a public office: your clients book a slot, get a reminder, and wait for their turn without sitting in the waiting room.

## Set up bookings

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create a service

In **Services**, create a **new service**: its name and the length of one appointment are enough. It can be booked at once, with you receiving clients Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00.

</Step>
<Step>

### Share the booking link

On the **Link and hours** page, tap **Copy link** or **Share**. Your clients pick a free slot, with no account and no call. Just below, **Change hours** adjusts them day by day, breaks included, then **Save hours**. Length and price are set in **Settings**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Turn on reminders

In **Appointment reminders**, a reminder goes out before each appointment on WhatsApp. When SMS is offered, tick **If WhatsApp can't deliver, send an SMS** for clients without WhatsApp or a data connection.

</Step>
</Steps>

## The day line

Appointments and walk-ins wait in one line. Show the **day line entrance QR** (in the service's **Advanced options**) at reception: clients scan it, take their own ticket and follow their place live on their phone.

At the counter, your staff open the **day line** from their link, call the next client ("It's your turn, counter 4") and add walk-ins.

<Callout title="How much does it cost?">
  The Solo plan is free forever for one person who receives clients. For a team, the [simulator](/en/simulator) gives the exact price.
</Callout>
