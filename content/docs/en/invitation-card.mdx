---
title: Create and share an invitation card
description: Post a card in your WhatsApp groups, let everyone answer, and count the yeses.
---

The invitation card is for when you have no guest list: a birthday, a party, a club celebration. You post one card, everyone answers in one tap, and every "yes" gets a QR ticket.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the event

In **Events**, create your event with its name, date, time and place. That is what the card will show.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open the invitation

In the event, open **Open invitation**, then start a shareable invitation. Under **Customize the card**, pick its style: Elegant, Modern or Festive, with your photo or your color.

</Step>
<Step>

### Set the answers

Still under **Customize the card**, set how many plus-ones each person may bring and the deadline to answer. **Show how many are coming** shows “23 people are coming” on the card from 5 people: only the number appears, never a name. **Let guests invite someone** offers, after “I'm coming”, to pass the card to someone forgotten: on by default when places are unlimited, off when you set a limit. The **Take answers** switch stays in view at the top to open or close the card in one tap.

</Step>
<Step>

### Publish the event

The card only accepts answers once the event is published. The **Publish** button tells you what is still missing.

</Step>
<Step>

### Share the card

From **Your card**, on a phone, tap **Share…**: the card image goes with the ready message, to WhatsApp, Facebook, Messenger, Telegram or SMS. The **WhatsApp**, **Facebook** and **Telegram** buttons send the link, whose preview shows the card; you can also copy the link. **Download the card** offers three sizes: **For a status** (vertical, full screen), **For a post** and **Landscape**. When you change the card, links shared afterwards show the new preview.

On the card, your guests see the countdown (“In 39 days”), tap the place for directions, and once they answer add the date to their calendar (Google Calendar or any other, with a reminder the day before).

Under **Your card**, **Voice message** lets you say the invitation in your own voice, 30 seconds at most: tap **Record**, listen back, add the message's words if you like, then **Use this message**. Guests listen at the top of the card; nothing downloads until they tap play. Without a microphone, **Import a file** takes a recording you already have.

Below the card, **Where answers come from** counts, for each network, the shares, the visits of the card and the answers: every shared link carries its network, and so does the image's QR code. You see where your people answer most, without anyone being tracked: only numbers are kept.

![The Open invitation tab: expected answers, the card link and the share buttons](/guides/en/open-invitation.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>

## Follow the answers

Answers arrive live in **Responses**: who is coming, who is unsure, who is not, and with how many plus-ones. Every "yes" gets a QR ticket, checked at the door as described in [Check guests in](./check-in).
