---
title: Create and share an invitation card
description: Post a card in your WhatsApp groups, let everyone answer, and count the yeses.
---

The invitation card is for when you have no guest list: a birthday, a party, a club celebration. You post one card, everyone answers in one tap, and every "yes" gets a QR ticket.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the event

In **Events**, create your event with its name, date, time and place. That is what the card will show.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open the invitation

In the event, open **Open invitation**, then start a shareable invitation. Pick the **card style**: Elegant, Modern or Festive, with your photo or your colour.

</Step>
<Step>

### Set the answers

In **Settings**, set how many plus-ones each person may bring, the number of places and the deadline to answer.

</Step>
<Step>

### Publish the event

The card only accepts answers once the event is published. The **Publish** button tells you what is still missing.

</Step>
<Step>

### Share the card

From **Your card**, share the link in your WhatsApp groups and statuses, or show its QR code. The card's preview shows right in the conversation.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Follow the answers

Answers arrive live in **Responses**: who is coming, who is unsure, who is not, and with how many plus-ones. Every "yes" gets a QR ticket, checked at the door as described in [Check guests in](./check-in).
